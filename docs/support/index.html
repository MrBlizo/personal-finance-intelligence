Support PFI

PFI is free and open source, and it stays that way: no ads, no account, no paid tier. I build it in my spare time. If it saves you time or money, a small contribution helps me keep improving it.

Ko-fi

A one-time tip by card or PayPal, no GitHub account needed.

Free ways to help

Rate PFI in the Microsoft Store, star it on GitHub, or tell a friend. Ratings help other people find it.

What a donation does and doesn't do

A donation unlocks nothing in the app: every feature is for everyone, whether you give or not. PFI doesn't know who donated, and your financial data never leaves your PC. Payments are handled entirely by Ko-fi and PayPal.

Found a bug or want a feature? Open an issue on GitHub; that helps as much as money.
